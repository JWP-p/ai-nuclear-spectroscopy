## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-11

### Added

- Provenance-first NNDC ENSDF/XUNDL retrieval client.
- Inspectable ENSDF identification, level, and gamma subset parser.
- Source-local F-D-G cascade screening and deterministic ranking.
- Transparent experimental-statistics assessment.
- Structured evidence/counterevidence review protocol with a human gate.
- Four-region subtraction, signed iterative centroids, PRD covariance, and GCD lifetime estimation.
- Fully fictional end-to-end demonstration and machine-readable evidence artifacts.
- Test suite, CI, public-surface audit, governance documentation, prompts, and citation metadata.
