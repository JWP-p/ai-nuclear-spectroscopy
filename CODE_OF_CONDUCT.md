# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, evidence-centered community in which researchers, software contributors, students, and scientific-agent developers can work together without harassment or exclusion.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims, methods, and code without attacking people.
- Distinguish measured evidence, inference, uncertainty, and speculation.
- Credit data evaluators, software authors, experiment teams, and reviewers appropriately.
- Welcome questions and explain specialist terminology when practical.
- Respect confidentiality, collaboration policy, embargoes, and data-use conditions.
- Disclose relevant limitations and correct mistakes visibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, credential exposure, deliberate fabrication, plagiarism, undisclosed manipulation of evidence, and retaliation against good-faith review are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior threatens people, scientific integrity, or project safety.
