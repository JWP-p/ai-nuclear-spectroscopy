# Authorship and AI Assistance

## Maintainer

PanDa is the project creator and primary maintainer. The public implementation, examples, prompts, documentation, and release structure were prepared from the maintainer's nuclear-spectroscopy workflow and published under the Apache License 2.0.

## AI-assisted development

OpenAI GPT/Codex was used as an interactive coding, refactoring, testing, and editorial assistant. The maintainer supplied the scientific objective and domain workflow, selected the public-release boundary, reviewed the resulting structure, and authorized publication.

AI assistance included:

- organizing reusable scientific concepts into a public package;
- drafting and refactoring code under explicit method constraints;
- generating synthetic fixtures and tests;
- checking for private paths, credentials, and unsupported public claims; and
- shaping documentation and prompt contracts for human–AI scientific review.

The released material was reviewed through tests and a public-surface audit. Those checks do not make an AI system a legal author, copyright owner, scientific approver, or guarantor of physical correctness. Human contributors remain responsible for every contribution they submit and every scientific claim they promote.

## Originality statement

The maintainer states that the released code and prompts were independently developed by the maintainer with GPT assistance and were not copied from a third-party codebase. General mathematical relations and public data-interface concepts are documented as methods and facts; third-party data, articles, figures, and private experimental artifacts are not redistributed.

If later review identifies a missing attribution or incompatible material, report it so the maintainer can investigate and correct the public record.
